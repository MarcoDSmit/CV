From clinical questions
to useful research.

Working across South Africa and Ireland has shaped my interest in infectious diseases, diagnostic uncertainty, and the practical challenges of delivering good care.

I’m motivated by research that helps clinicians make better decisions and helps patients understand their care. My AI interests grow from that same commitment: evaluating what works, recognising limitations, and keeping the patient at the centre.

Selected work

Research & interests

AI research · First author

Mapping AI in infectious diseases

I initiated and led a scoping review and evidence map of diagnostic, therapeutic, and educational applications in infectious diseases and clinical microbiology. Presented at IDSI 2026; manuscript in preparation.

Patient communication · First author

Language models and clearer patient information

Research on using large language models to improve the readability of patient information leaflets. Poster presented at WADEM 2025.

Infectious diseases · Clinical research

Safer, more effective infection care

Work spanning outpatient parenteral antimicrobial therapy (OPAT), prosthetic joint infections, antimicrobial stewardship, and electronic HIV medication prescribing. My MSc dissertation evaluated OPAT safety and cost-effectiveness.
